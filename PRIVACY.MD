# Privacy Policy — Infinite Elo

**Last updated:** 2 September 2026
**Applies to:** Infinite Elo on iOS, iPadOS, macOS, Android, Windows, Linux and Steam
**Contact:** daequanbritt@yahoo.com

## The short version

Infinite Elo does not collect any data about you. None. There is no account,
no login, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no network
connection of any kind. Nothing you do in the game is transmitted anywhere,
because the game has no means of transmitting anything.

## What the game stores

Two files, on your own device, readable only by you:

| File | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| `verdant_vale_save.json` | Your progress: position, level, elo rating, badges, cards, inventory and playtime. |
| `verdant_vale_prefs.json` | Your settings: volume levels, screen orientation, control-pad colour, vibration strength. |

They are written to the private application-data folder the operating system
gives the game:

- **iOS / iPadOS and Android** — the app's sandboxed storage, which no other
  app can read.
- **Windows** — `%APPDATA%\Infinite Elo`
- **macOS** — `~/Library/Application Support/Infinite Elo`
- **Linux** — `~/.local/share/Infinite Elo`

Neither file leaves your device. Deleting the game deletes them. There is no
copy anywhere else, and no way for us to reach them.

**Steam Cloud.** If you play on Steam and have Steam Cloud enabled, Valve may
sync your save file between your own machines. That is a feature of Steam
operating on your behalf, under
[Valve's privacy policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/), and
you can switch it off in the game's Steam properties. We never receive that
data.

## What the game does not do

To be specific, rather than merely reassuring — Infinite Elo does **not**:

- create accounts, ask for a login, or accept any text you type about yourself;
- read device identifiers, advertising IDs, your IDFA, or your contacts,
  photos, microphone, camera, calendar or location;
- contain advertising, ad SDKs, or any third-party SDK whatsoever;
- track you across apps or websites, which is why it never presents an App
  Tracking Transparency prompt;
- gather analytics, telemetry, crash reports or usage statistics;
- offer in-app purchases or take payment of any kind.

On Android, the released build **requests no permissions at all**. It does not
hold the `INTERNET` permission, so it is not merely a promise that the game
does not phone home — the operating system will not let it.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages and, collecting nothing from anyone, collects
nothing from children. It falls outside COPPA, the GDPR's provisions on
children's data, and the Google Play Families data requirements, because there
is no data to which they could apply.

## Your rights

Privacy law gives you rights to access, correct, export and delete the personal
data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to give you,
correct, or erase. Your save file is already entirely in your hands: it is on
your device, and deleting the game removes it.

If you want to be certain, delete the app; on desktop, delete the folder listed
above.

## Changes

If a future version of the game ever collects anything — it has no plans to —
this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be
described in the game's UPDATES panel.
